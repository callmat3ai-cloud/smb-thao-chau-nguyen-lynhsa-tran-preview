Our Philosophy

Creating space for slow, honest self-care.

Thao Chau Nguyen and Lynhsa Tran founded their Monterey Park sanctuary on the belief that nail care should be an restorative pause in your day, not a rushed errand.

Taking inspiration from Scandinavian designs of simplicity, utility, and comfort, the studio creates a calming space with warm lighting, botanical scents, and clean lines. We work exclusively with high-quality, organic, and toxin-free formulations to safeguard the wellness of your natural nails.
